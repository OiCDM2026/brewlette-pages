Delete your Bréwlette data

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Bréwlette doesn't ask you to sign up, so there is no email address or password to remember. Your data is tied to an anonymous account created on your device when you first open the app (iPhone or Android).

Delete it in the app (recommended)

  1. Open Bréwlette.
  2. On the home screen, tap Settings.
  3. Tap Delete my account, then confirm.

This permanently:

  • removes you from every group you're in,
  • deletes your saved drink order and your display name,
  • deletes your push notification token, and
  • erases your anonymous account.

It can't be undone. Spin results that a group has already recorded show only a display name, and they are removed along with your membership.

If you've uninstalled the app or can't open it

Email craigdmarks@icloud.com from any address and say that you'd like your Bréwlette data deleted. Because accounts are anonymous, please tell us the name you used and the name of one group you were in (or its invite code), so we can find your data. We'll delete it and reply to confirm.

What stays on your device

Quick spin player lists and the spin history shown in the app are stored only on your phone, never sent to us. They are removed when you delete the app or tap Delete my account.
