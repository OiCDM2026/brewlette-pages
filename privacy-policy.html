Bréwlette Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Bréwlette (on iPhone and Android) is a simple app for deciding who makes the next round of drinks in a group. This page explains what information it collects and why.

What we collect

  • Your display name — the name you enter when you set up the app, shown to other members of your groups.
  • Your drink preference — the drink, type, milk, sugar, and notes you set, shown to whoever's brewing.
  • An anonymous account identifier — created automatically when you open the app, so the app can recognise you across sessions without asking you to sign up or provide an email address.
  • Group membership data — which groups you're in, your "ready" status, and how many times you've brewed or been included in a spin.
  • A push notification token, if you allow notifications, so the app can tell you when a round's been decided.

Some things stay only on your phone and are never sent to us: the players you enter in Quick spin, and the spin history shown in the app.

We do not collect your email address, phone number, precise location, or any payment information. We don't use advertising, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.

Why we collect it

Everything above exists purely to make the app work: matching you to the right groups, showing your drink order to whoever's brewing, deciding who's "ready" to be included in a spin, and notifying you of results. We don't use your data for anything else, and we don't sell or share it with third parties.

Where it's stored

Your data is stored using Google Firebase (Firestore and Firebase Authentication), hosted in Google Cloud's europe-west2 (London) region.

Your choices

  • Leaving a group removes you from that group's member list; other members can no longer see your order.
  • Deleting your account — available in-app under Settings → "Delete my account" — permanently removes you from every group you're in, deletes your saved drink order and push notification token, and erases your account entirely. This can't be undone. You can also read how to delete your data, including if you've uninstalled the app.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date above will change too.
