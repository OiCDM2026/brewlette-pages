Bréwlette Support

Having trouble with Bréwlette, or want to report a bug or suggest a feature?

Email: craigdmarks@icloud.com

I can't join a group

Double-check the 6-letter invite code with whoever created the group — codes never include the letters 0, O, 1, or I, to avoid mix-ups.

The wheel won't spin

At least two people in the group need to mark themselves "ready" before a spin can happen.

I want to leave a group

Open the group, tap the sliders icon (My order) at the top, then choose Leave group — this removes you from that group only; your other groups are unaffected.

I want to delete my account

Open the app, go to Settings, and choose "Delete my account." This permanently removes you from every group and can't be undone. More details.
